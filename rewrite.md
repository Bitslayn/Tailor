# Structure

Everything should be structured how they'd work in the UI; Outfits and garment sets that can easily be swapped out as well as customized.

- Outfits: Quickly switch clothing using preset outfits
- Clothing: Mix and match different clothing

Group > Garment > Customizations

- Group: The clothing groups, that being Shirts, Pants, Hats, etc.
- Garment: The piece of clothing, Croptop, Sweater, etc.
- Customization: The customization applied to the clothing such as colors, lengths, designs, and alternative styles
